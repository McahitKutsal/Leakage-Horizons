## S6. Two calibrations and a control the main text points at

### S6.1 Dating noise against the declared-resolution stratum

The generator of Section 6.2 can make a register *wrong* rather than *early*, by displacing a declared share of coded dates one period late. This is the synthetic counterpart of a register that assigns dates by rule inside a five-year block.

Under that displacement, focal isolation's presence is exactly the displaced share at one period and zero beyond. A displaced share of a tenth gives a presence of 0.0667 at one period; three tenths gives 0.3000. The mapping is therefore close to linear in the displaced share over this range, which is what makes it usable as a bound in the audit procedure of Section 9.

Corpus A's declared-resolution stratum reads 0.1419 at one period. Read through this mapping, that corresponds to about one event in seven in that stratum carrying a coded date one period late. The reading is a quantitative bridge between the testbed and the one place in corpus A where back-dating is measured rather than entailed.

### S6.2 A heterogeneous width makes the estimand a choice

Redraw the back-dating width per event from the trade register's coded gaps. The mean presence at lead time $\Delta$ is then the share of events whose width is at least $\Delta$. A rule that waits for that mean to reach zero therefore returns the maximum width, while a rule thresholding it at one half returns the median. Both are exact for their own estimand in all twelve settings tested.

We report the maximum-based rule in the main text, because one non-zero lead time is enough to widen the horizon. That is also why the rule is conservative, and why a single badly coded event moves it.

### S6.3 Ambient administrative content is not a trace of the event

Section 7.3 counts only the off-focal dyads that the focal event caused. The remainder — administrative dyads that exist whether or not the focal event happened — reach as far as thirteen periods and must be reported separately rather than added to the horizon.

Across the whole 840-corpus sweep, the mean reach of that ambient content is 1.0053 periods for positives against 0.8396 for matched negatives drawn from the same group at the same period. The share of candidates carrying any such dyad is 0.1401 against 0.1359. Those are the same number to within a rounding of the share.

An administrative dyad that positives and negatives carry alike is not a trace of the event, and a diagnostic that read it as one would be reporting exactly the artefact this paper is about. That is why the caused part is separated and the ambient part reported beside it.
